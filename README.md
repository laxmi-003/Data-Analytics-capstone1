## Dataset
- Total Employees: 1470
- File: clean HR Analytics.xlsx

## Tools Used
- Excel (Pivot Table, Slicers, Dashboard)
- Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn)

## My Excel Dashboard - HR Attrition Analysis 2024

I created an Interactive Dashboard in Excel.

### Key KPIs:
- Total Employee: 1470
- Attrition Rate: 16.09%
- Average Age: 36.9 Years

### Dashboard Features:
1. Slicers for filtering: Age Group, Job Role, Department, Gender, Attrition
2. Charts:
   - Monthly Income by Department and Job Role
   - Department wise Attrition
   - Overtime Analysis (Donut Chart)
   - Business Travel Analysis
   - Age Group Analysis
   - Gender Distribution (60% Male, 40% Female)

### Key Insights from Dashboard:
- Sales and R&D departments have highest attrition
- Age group 26-35 has the highest number of employees leaving
- Employees who do Overtime leave more
- Average age of employees is 36.9

## Conclusion
Young employees and those doing overtime are more likely to leave. Company should focus on work-life balance.
